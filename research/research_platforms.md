# Research lane: data/map/ranking short video (Reels + TikTok), 2025-2026

Evidence quality warning: almost nothing here is platform-official or measured on map/ranking videos. Sources are marketing blogs (OpusClip is a vendor, numbers mostly unsourced). Rules marked (INFERENCE) are my design calls, not sourced. Not found / unverified: countdown order test, any @datawrapper/@visualcapitalist/@blockclubchi reel data, any civic-video data, any official Meta/TikTok pixel safe zone.

## Do this (14 rules)
1. Length 15-25 s. Educational Reels 15-30 s; entertainment 7-15 s. TikTok 21-34 s had highest completion (62% vs 48% for >60 s, vendor-reported). Pick ~20 s. https://www.opus.pro/blog/ideal-instagram-reels-length , https://www.opus.pro/blog/tiktok-length-format-retention-data
2. Hook inside the first 1.5 s, and make it work on mute. Instagram's own advice: hook in first 3 s, watch "View Rate" (share who pass 3 s). https://www.opus.pro/blog/ideal-instagram-reels-length ; Instagram point via search summary only (unverified-by-fetch).
3. Frame 1 must already show the blue map plus the stat as a title card ("Chicago's most dangerous wards to walk"). No logo intro, no fade-in. Intros lose 30-40% vs opening with value (vendor claim). https://www.opus.pro/blog/tiktok-length-format-retention-data
4. Change something on screen every 1.5-3 s (Reels) / 3-5 s (TikTok). With 5 reveals in ~20 s, one reveal every ~3 s fits. No static shot over 4 s. https://www.opus.pro/blog/ideal-instagram-reels-length , https://www.opus.pro/blog/tiktok-length-format-retention-data
5. Each text overlay on screen 1-2 s (Reels source) to at least 2 s (TikTok source). Use ~2 s per ward label; keep it to 3-6 words. https://www.opus.pro/blog/ideal-instagram-reels-length , https://www.opus.pro/blog/tiktok-length-format-retention-data
6. Burn in captions/labels; do not rely on sound. Claims of 60-85% muted viewing are unsourced; accurate captions ~+12% retention is vendor-reported. https://www.opus.pro/blog/tiktok-length-format-retention-data
7. Safe zone, design to the union: top 290 px, bottom 675 px, left 120 px, right 190 px, and keep the lower-right 300 px (from y=840) free of anything key. Source numbers: Reels 269/672/65/65; TikTok 240/660/120/120 + 300 px button block from y=840. https://www.upload-post.com/tools/safe-zone-checker/ (third-party, ad guidance, varies by device). Reels percent version 14% top, 35% bottom, 6% sides, buttons over rightmost ~21%: https://www.hopperhq.com/blog/instagram-reel-size/ . TikTok alt: top 150, bottom 350-400, right 120: https://www.trymypost.com/blog/tiktok-ad-specs-2026-safe-zones . Verify in each app's preview before final.
8. Make the cover a deliberate frame: Reels grid crops to 3:4 (1080x1440), cutting 240 px top and bottom. Keep title and key content inside that box, 3-4 words, solid or dark backing. Set it in "Edit cover" before posting (cannot change after). https://www.hopperhq.com/blog/instagram-reel-size/
9. Hold the final frame 1-2 s and make the end connect to the first frame so it loops (under-15 s Reels especially). Rewatch counts as a ranking signal. https://www.opus.pro/blog/tiktok-length-format-retention-data , https://www.opus.pro/blog/ideal-instagram-reels-length . TikTok lists rewatch, comments, shares, captions, hashtags, sounds as recommendation signals: https://www.tiktok.com/support/faq_detail?id=7655285288050104852
10. Instagram weighs predicted reshares, full watches, likes, and audio-page visits. Design for shares ("send to someone in that ward") and full watch. https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained
11. Instagram reduces reach for low-res, watermarked, muted, bordered, mostly-text, or already-posted reels. So: export 1080x1920 clean, no TikTok watermark on the Reels upload, have some audio track, not a text slide show. https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained
12. Use 0-2 hashtags, keywords in the caption. Metricool (24M posts): posts with hashtags got 32% fewer views and 34% fewer interactions; Instagram indexes captions for search. Put "Chicago", "ward", "pedestrian safety" in caption text. https://marketing4ecommerce.net/en/what-works-on-instagram-in-2026/
13. Post on a steady cadence and push the comment prompt early: 2 of 3 Reel views and 61% of interactions come within 3 days of posting. Specific optimal cadence is NOT established (SocialPilot: no magic number). https://marketing4ecommerce.net/en/what-works-on-instagram-in-2026/
14. Original audio / own mix is attributed to you and remixable; trending audio mainly helps discovery, retention depends more on content and pacing. Use quiet bed + tick sound, not a trending song (INFERENCE). https://creators.instagram.com/blog/the-latest-with-instagram , https://www.opus.pro/blog/ideal-instagram-reels-length
15. Put a payoff early: payoffs inside the first 15 s held ~20% more retention than end-loaded ones (vendor-reported, TikTok). So tease the #1 ward in the hook, then count. https://www.opus.pro/blog/tiktok-length-format-retention-data
16. Use trial reels (Instagram) to test two hooks on non-followers before showing followers. https://creators.instagram.com/blog/the-latest-with-instagram

## My design calls (INFERENCE, no source tested these)
- Countdown: show 5 worst (#5 to #1), tease "#1 is in the hook" without naming it. Top 10 would be too long at 15-30 s (10 reveals x 2 s = 20 s with no hook or end). Keep the full 50-ward list on the site via CTA.
- Hook lines (style: stat + stake + local):
  1. "Chicago's 5 most dangerous wards to walk. #1 might be yours."
  2. "50 wards. Only 5 are this bad for people on foot."
  3. "One ward had more pedestrian and bike crashes than any other in 2025."
  4. "Do you live in a deadly ward? Check the map."
  5. "Same city. This ward is 4x as dangerous to walk in." (use only if the real ratio holds)
- CTA that drives comments: "Where did your ward land? Comment your ward number." and "Which ward should we rank next?" Add "Look up your ward: wardwise.org" in caption (links in Reels captions do not click, so say it in the video bio).
- Local/civic: no fetched evidence. Closest: SOCi (vendor) says location-level posts beat corporate ones, and a hyperlocal-news survey (n=2,289) found neighborhood involvement raises sharing, in-person more than online. Those came from search snippets only, not fetched = unverified.

## Detail by question
1. Length/loop: Reels cap is now 3 min and non-followers can see reels up to 3 min (https://creators.instagram.com/blog/the-latest-with-instagram), but these are maximums. Opus retention bands: 7-15 s 60-80%, 15-30 s 40-60%, >45 s under 30% (author experience, unsourced). Total watch time can favor longer clips only if retention holds (40 s @60% = 24 s vs 20 s @70% = 14 s, TikTok source). Loop: end frame back to start under 15 s; clear payoff in last 2 s for longer.
2. Hook: sources agree on bold moment, stat, or question at frame 1 (https://virlo.ai/blog/why-the-first-few-seconds-matter). The "60% higher retention" figure there is vendor-level, unverified. Their example lines are about video-making, not usable; mine above are my wording.
3. Countdown: NO fetched evidence on count-up vs reveal-first. Only blog claim (search snippet, unfetched) that ranked lists perform across niches and give repeated hooks. A/B this with trial reels.
4. Safe zones: see rule 7. Sources disagree (Outfy/FirstPier 108 top/320 bottom were seen only in a search summary; unverified). Reels centered safe box ~950x980 px (Hopper). Meta publishes percentages in its ad docs; I could not reach Meta's page.
5. Text: no source gives pixel font size. Only rule: legible at postage-stamp size (Hopper). INFERENCE: 64-80 px labels, rank numeral 200+ px, white on dark blue or dark on pale, 3-6 words per screen, center or upper-middle, not mostly-text (penalized).
6. Caption/cover/cadence: rules 8, 12, 13. Instagram says reposts, watermarks, mostly-reshare accounts are less recommended; put keywords in the caption not the comments (search summary of creators pages; unfetched).
7. Local/civic: unverified, see above.
